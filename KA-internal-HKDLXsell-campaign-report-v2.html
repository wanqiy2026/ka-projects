Behavioral Insights

📈 Strategy 3 drove the clearest campaign spike

  • Launched Aug 10; platform orders spiked to 1,184 on Aug 11 — the campaign's single largest day
  • That's ~+78% vs Aug 10 (665 orders), and 2× the pre-campaign daily baseline
  • 3-tier structure (vs S2's 2 tiers) likely encouraged basket upsizing to hit higher discount thresholds
  • Budget extended to 80,000 CNY and campaign extended to Sep 12 — 27,377 CNY (34.2%) still available, still actively running

🎯 ~100% of Aug coupon orders went to HK Disneyland

  • 1,253 of 1,253 Aug 1–31 coupon orders were directly attributed to HK Disneyland
  • All three strategies converge to 100% Disney share in August — their targeted currencies (TWD/MYR/THB/HKD/KRW) align almost exclusively with Disney buyers on platform
  • S3 generated the most Disney GMV despite launching latest — 681K CNY vs 594K (S1) and 482K (S2)
  • Most popular product: Party of Two 1-Day Ticket (Free HKD 80 Dining Voucher) — TWD buyers lead

💰 Discount efficiency: 6.5–7.8% of GMV

  • Discount cost as % of Disney GMV generated: S1 6.6% · S2 6.9% · S3 7.8%
  • ~40% coupon-to-order conversion rate across all three strategies (vs 15–25% industry benchmark)
  • Thresholds are well-calibrated — ~65% of users hit the entry tier, 35% upsell to higher tiers
  • TWD exception: middle tier (6K→-600) is the most used — product price naturally lands there for 2-person tickets

👤 Coupons attracted smaller-group / individual bookers

  • Avg tickets per order fell from 2.7 → 1.9 during the campaign vs pre-campaign
  • HK market saw biggest drop (3.2 → 1.9 tickets/order), US also declined
  • TW, KR, MY saw tickets/order hold or increase — consistent with family/group travel from those markets
  • Signals new/casual individual bookers entering via coupons — relevant for new customer acquisition story

📈 Attach rate improved YoY across all push markets

  • Corrected metric: GBA-bound flyers (to HKG/SZX/CAN/MFM) who also bought HK Disneyland, same month
  • TW: 0.51% → 0.88% (+73% YoY) · TH: 0.55% → 0.95% (+73%) · MY: 0.09% → 0.37% (+311%)
  • TH July 2026 was an outlier spike at 1.42% (Thai school holidays + S2/S3 launch); Aug normalised back to 0.95% — in line with Jun 2026 (0.94%) and well above Aug 2025 (0.55%)
  • JP improved +130% YoY with only S1 — platform-level tailwinds are real, campaign markets sit on top of that

🌏 TWD is the dominant market across all three coupons

  • Taiwan (TWD) is the top or second-largest currency in every strategy: 55.5% of S2, 18.8% of S3, 15.6% of S1
  • HKD is the other consistent anchor — 19.1% (S1), 38.4% (S3)
  • S1's 16-currency spread dilutes any single market's share by design; S2/S3 concentrate tightly around 3 core markets each

📉 Strategy 2 momentum has fully tapered

  • Near-zero new saves since mid-August; activity has largely stopped
  • Peaked around Aug 7–8 (~100 daily saves), then steadily declined
  • 64.6% of budget spent with no remaining organic pull — the addressable audience has been captured
  • S3 effectively replaced S2's role with broader currency coverage and tighter tiers
